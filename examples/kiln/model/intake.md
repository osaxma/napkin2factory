# Intake

**What and why.** A small front-loading electric test kiln for glaze tests and small pieces:
a 200 × 200 × 200 mm chamber, 1200 °C class. **Who.** A hobby potter at home; no electrical
or thermal background. **Envelope.** Bench alcove about 800 × 800 mm; the kiln sits on its
own stand. **Targets.** 1200 °C in about four hours; useful volume at least 8 L.
**Environment and safety.** Mains powered; surfaces people touch must stay below 60 °C; the
controller must stay cool; a dedicated circuit by an electrician; door procedure.
**Budget and quantity.** One unit, under about 1500 USD in parts. **Manufacturing.** A local
sheet-metal shop and a refractory supplier; the owner assembles. **What exists.** Nothing;
region not stated (230 V assumed, DEC-006).
