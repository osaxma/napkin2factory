# Intake

**Customer Intent:**
"I test springs and plastic snap-fits on my bench. I want a small motorized press that pushes up to
about 200 kg, measures force and travel accurately, plugs into a normal US wall socket, stops dead
when I hit the red button or open the shield, and that I can lift onto the bench on my own."

**Envelope & Limits:**
- Rated compressive load: 2000 N (204 kgf)
- Test cycles over life: N = 1.0 × 10⁶
- Frame deflection at load axis: ≤ 0.20 mm
- Vertical daylight: ≥ 250 mm
- Clear width between members: ≥ 200 mm
- Actuator stroke: 200 mm
- Bench footprint: ≤ 400 mm wide × 350 mm deep
- Total mass: ≤ 25 kg (one-person lift)
- Parts cost: ≤ 2400 USD
- Supply: 120 V, 60 Hz, NEMA 5-15 receptacle, input current ≤ 3 A
