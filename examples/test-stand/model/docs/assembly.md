# Assembly

The stand comes in three units. Put them together in this order.

1. **Console.** The tray carries the four feet, the inlet, the power supply and the controller;
   the lid carries the E-stop.
2. **Portal frame.** Bolt the base beam to the lid at the column feet, the two columns to the
   base beam, and the crosshead to the columns with two M8 bolts in each end. Hang the actuator
   under the crosshead, then the load cell and the upper platen on the rod. Screw the lower
   platen to the base beam. Fit the two limit switches.
3. **Guard.** Fix the shroud to the columns, hang the door on its two hinges and fit the
   interlock switch.

Run the harness through the grommet in the lid, then connect the wiring as the wiring pages say.
