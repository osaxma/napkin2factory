# Intake

**What and why.** A steel L-bracket to hold a wooden shelf carrying up to 10 kg, two
brackets per shelf. **Who.** The owner installs it at home with a drill. **Envelope.**
Must fit behind a 150 mm shelf lip. **Targets.** 10 kg per bracket, under 0.5 kg, under
30 USD each at a quantity of 10. **Safety.** Sharp edges before deburring; wall anchors
are the owner's responsibility. **Manufacturing.** Any local sheet-metal shop.
