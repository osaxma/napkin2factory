# Intake

Summary of what the owner asked for (SPEC.md section 1 has the figures and where each comes from).

- **What:** a solid oak table for the workshop that doubles as a dining table, on a black steel base.
- **Size:** about 1.6 m by 0.8 m, normal table height (top surface 750 mm above the floor).
- **Loads:** takes a 150 kg load in the middle without visibly sagging (at most 2.5 mm); must not
  tip when someone leans or sits on the edge (50 kg at the middle of any edge); a vise and tools
  (50 kg) may stay on it.
- **Moving it:** two people must be able to move it; the owner's limit is 70 kg assembled.
- **Wood:** the top must be free to move with the seasons (30 % to 70 % relative humidity) so it
  does not crack.
- **Room:** indoor, about 21 degC; no stretcher or rail between the legs below the apron.
- **Budget and quantity:** under 1600 USD, one table. Prices are US, as of 2026-11.
- **Power:** none (no electrical domain).
- **Already exists:** nothing; the owner chose the oak top with a steel apron frame from three
  napkin directions (concepts/ keeps all three).
