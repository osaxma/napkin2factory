# Scripted intake: wall bracket for a 10 kg monitor

Role-played owner transcript handed to a fresh-context subagent together with the product
folder's `CLAUDE.md` and the `n2f-pm` skill text (GRAND_PLAN §1.3 item 6, M7 exit). The
subagent runs inside a freshly `n2f init`ed folder and must end with a model that passes
`n2f validate` and `n2f lint` and builds a first silhouette with `n2f build --target geometry`.

---

**Owner:** I want a bracket to hang my monitor on the wall. It's a 27 inch monitor, about 10 kg
with its stand removed. The back has the standard four screw holes, 100 mm apart in a square.

**PM:** (asks who uses it / environment)

**Owner:** Just me, in my home office. Plasterboard wall with wooden studs behind it. Indoors,
nothing special.

**PM:** (asks about size and position)

**Owner:** I don't want it sticking out more than about 6 cm from the wall. The monitor should
sit at roughly eye height, but I'll pick the exact spot when I drill. It doesn't need to tilt
or swivel. Fixed is fine.

**PM:** (asks about targets)

**Owner:** It must hold the 10 kg monitor with plenty of margin — I'd be happy if it could take
double that. I don't want it to flex visibly.

**PM:** (asks about safety)

**Owner:** The main worry is it falling off the wall onto the desk, or me cutting myself on a
sharp edge while fitting it.

**PM:** (asks about budget and quantity)

**Owner:** One, maybe two. Under 25 dollars each would be nice. No rush.

**PM:** (asks about manufacturing)

**Owner:** There's a small metal shop in town that does laser cutting and bending. Steel is
fine, I'd paint it black.

**PM:** (asks what exists)

**Owner:** Nothing yet. I have M4 screws for the monitor's holes. I'll buy wall anchors for
the studs separately.
