## 0. Why this release
Through v0.6 the kiln was the proving ground, so the calc kit, the data tables, the kit and the
ceremony are kiln-shaped. v0.7 makes a 5-part oak-and-steel table and an 11-part motorized test
stand as natural and as honest as the kiln, and builds the three-tier benchmark suite every
later track is tested against. Its headline numbers are the §2.1 measures (the first
measurement sets the targets) and the count of `core/` files each milestone touched (road §5.5).

## 1. Mission, non-goals, definition of done
**Mission:** structural physics (members and joints), everyday materials and processes with
dated regional costs, the kit and interfaces a table needs, ceremony derived from the model,
supply region, and `examples/table` + `examples/test-stand` with worked `SPEC.md` and an oracle.

**Non-goals (stay in `BACKLOG.md`):** FEA (B-0040); manufacturer profiles (B-0038); tolerance
stacks (B-0039); imperial units on drawings (the report shows the owner's unit first; drawings
stay in mm, W8); live draft ECRs and undo (Track B); the workbench (Track C); MCP (Track D).

**Definition of done (the macOS host plus the CI matrix, S-001):**
1. `calckit/structural.py` and `calckit/joints.py` cover every row of Track A §3.1, each function
   with cited reference cases; `calckit/data/load_cases.yaml` holds every default factor with its
   source; `mypy --strict` clean.
2. One materials table (W2) with every Track A §3.2 material; four new processes; every cost
   band carries region, currency and date.
3. The frame kit, the panel kit and the four interface kinds of §3.3 build and check.
4. The generic `structural` screen rejects and promotes the benchmark cards for the reasons
   their `SPEC.md` gives.
5. Ceremony is derived (W6); the table is `lite`, the test stand and the kiln `standard`; a
   hazard added to a `lite` product raises it with a notice.
6. A table without the product's region fails loudly (W7); the test stand runs on 120 V / 60 Hz.
7. `examples/table` and `examples/test-stand` build green from a clean clone; each has a
   `SPEC.md` with its worked hand calculation, re-derived by a second agent of another vendor,
   and a `SPEC.oracle.yaml`; the kiln has a retrofitted `SPEC.md`; `tests/e2e/test_benchmarks.py`
   checks all three against their oracles.
8. Gates I (engineering and honesty) and J (UX, owner persona) closed with blockers fixed; the
   spec reproduction trial passed by cold agents of at least two vendors (§4.3 of the roadmap);
   the §2.1 measures recorded; a health review (S-190); version 0.7.0, pins `>=0.7,<0.8`.
9. Every v0.6 product (the kiln, bracket, enclosure, the v0.6 fixtures) opens and builds
   unchanged except for the derived-ceremony report omissions, re-goldened with a logged reason.

## 2. Locked decisions (W-series)

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| W1 | **Two pure modules.** `calckit/structural.py` (sections, beams, columns, bending stress, tip-over, fastener group shear) and `calckit/joints.py` (insert and screw pull-out, joint moment, racking, wood creep, wood movement, fatigue). Pint quantities in and out, every function with `CASES` from a cited handbook (Roark, Shigley, the USDA Wood Handbook, a catalogue), `[verify]` where the source is a catalogue. Default factors of safety per check and load kind live in `calckit/data/load_cases.yaml`, each cited; a requirement may tighten a factor, never loosen it (lint) | Joints fail before members in furniture and frames; two files let two workers build in parallel; a factor without a source is an invented number |
| W2 | **One materials table.** `calckit/data/materials.yaml` gains `youngs_modulus`, `yield_strength` and a `moisture_basis` for woods, and becomes the only source of density: `geometry/materials.py` reads it instead of its own `DENSITIES` dict (rows merged, nothing lost). Printed plastics carry density and cost only; a structural check on a printed part fails unless the part declares a derated property with its source | Two tables drift; a printed part's strength depends on its print direction |
| W3 | **Dated regional costs.** Every cost band row has `region`, `currency`, `as_of` (a date); a band older than 24 months reads "stale" beside the figure on the board and in the parts list. New processes `woodworking`, `cnc-routing`, `laser-cutting`, `extrusion-cut` with their bands (`per_kg`, `per_m`, `per_m2`, `per_item`) | Costs without a date and place are not numbers (pillar 1) |
| W4 | **The `structural` screen is generic.** In `concepts/screens/structural.py`, registered beside the generic screens; its card fields are the package's words only (`point_load`, `distributed_load`, `edge_load`, `load_offset`, `max_deflection`, `cycles`, plus the member fields it needs: span, section, material, support kind). A product's own word for one (a throat, an overhang) is that product's alias in its folder (F9, F10). It reports deflection, yield factor, buckling and tip-over with the formula and source of each | The screen is how a napkin card is rejected for physics; an example's word in the package is how the kiln became the schema |
| W5 | **Interface kinds are data with a spec type.** `threaded-insert`, `wood-screw-pattern`, `t-slot-joint`, `hinge-pin` in `core/data/interface_kinds.yaml`, each spec typed as the bolt-pattern spec or an extension of it, so hole cutting and alignment work through the spec type (S-144) with no new core code path; a kind's own `check` (for example insert-to-substrate) lives with the structural pack | The S-144 seam exists for exactly this |
| W6 | **Ceremony is derived, raise-only.** `standard` when a domain with content reports a hazard (a new optional `Domain.hazards(product)` hook; electrical: mains, or above extra-low voltage), when more than one domain has content, or when a requirement carries a hazard tag (`hazard:<kind>`) whose kind the hazard table (`core/data/hazards.yaml`, cited) classes as raising (high temperature, moving machinery, stored energy, mains); `lite` otherwise. A `safety: true` requirement must name its hazard (lint), so the class follows from the model, not a wish. `n2f.yaml` `ceremony: standard` may raise it, never lower it. `n2f check` names the ceremony and, when it rose since the last build, what it now requires. `lite`: process-based default `tolerance_class` (a cited column on `processes.yaml`), a single `build-and-care.md` manual, empty sections omitted from the report; provenance, alignment and safety sign-offs never relax | Rigor follows what the product is; a tip-over requirement keeps its sign-off on a table |
| W7 | **Region is the product's.** `n2f.yaml` gains an optional `region` (the key the tables use: `EU`, `UK`, `US`, …); the supply's `region` defaults to it. The conductor, earthing and wire-colour tables and the cost bands are looked up by it; a table with no row for the region fails with the table's name and the regions it has, never a silent default. `earthing.yaml` gains its region column | The kiln's 50 Hz tables must never price or wire a 120 V stand |
| W8 | **Units of display.** `n2f.yaml` `units: metric \| imperial` (additive; `metric` default): the report's parameter, requirement and parts tables and the board show the owner's unit first and the other in brackets. Drawings and exported files stay in mm | Inputs in inches already work (pint); drawings in two units are a later decision |
| W9 | **Benchmarks are worked, then checked twice.** Each `SPEC.md` derives every figure it asserts from its stated dimensions with sources (road §4.1, the 30 % margin); a worker of another vendor re-derives the figures from the `SPEC.md` alone before the oracle is frozen, and a disagreement is resolved in the spec, not averaged. `SPEC.oracle.yaml` asserts outcomes, matched by what a requirement bounds (quantity kind and bound), never by part or requirement ids (road §4.2). The evaluator is `tests/benchmarks/oracle.py`, runnable against any product folder (`uv run python -m tests.benchmarks.oracle <product> <oracle>`), so a cold agent's product is judged by the same code as CI | The benchmark checks the tool against physics, not against itself |
| W10 | **The count.** M1, M2c and M4 are pack, screen and example work: their target is 0 `core/` files. M2b (interface data), M3a (ceremony) and M3b (region) are planned core work and list their core files in the commit body. A pack task that needs a core edit stops, commits `WIP:` naming the file and the reason, and the overseer opens a seam task with a `B-NEW` row (E10's rule) | The roadmap measures the contract by this count |
| W11 | **Backward compatible.** Every new field is optional with the old behaviour as default (`docs/schema-policy.md` rule 1, no bump); a v0.6 product opens and builds; the kiln, bracket and enclosure change only where derived ceremony omits empty sections (goldens regenerated with `make goldens REASON=…`) | Road §3.1: every v0.6 product opens in v1.0 |
| W12 | **Trial findings come first.** The v0.6 trial's blockers are tasks before or beside wave 1 (beside only when their files are disjoint from it); its concerns become `BACKLOG.md` rows or W-amendments here, recorded in M0's decision entry | Road §3: the trial may reorder the track |

## 3. Schema additions (all optional; shapes only)
```yaml
# n2f.yaml
region: US            # W7; the tables' key
units: imperial       # W8; display only
ceremony: standard    # W6; raise-only, absent = derived

# model/requirements.yaml — a safety requirement names its hazard (W6)
- id: REQ-005
  safety: true
  tags: [hazard:tip-over]

# a concept card (W4): the structural screen's generic fields
loads: {point_load: 1471.5 N, edge_load: 490.5 N, load_offset: 150 mm, max_deflection: 2.5 mm}
members: [{role: top, kind: beam, span: 1400 mm, section: {rect: [800 mm, 30 mm]}, material: white-oak, support: simple}]

# calckit/data/cost_bands.yaml (W3)
- {process_class: woodworking, unit: per_kg, low: 9, high: 22, currency: USD, region: US, as_of: 2026-11, verify: true, source: …}
```

## 6. `make check` growth
The calc and screen units run in `check-fast`. The table and the test stand build in `make check`
from prebuilt copies (S-196); the kiln's benchmark runs in `check-full`. Record `make check`'s
time at each wave boundary; the fallback is S-196's: move the slowest e2e files behind `-m full`.

## 7. Risks
| Risk | Watch | Fallback |
|---|---|---|
| A benchmark number is wrong and the oracle enshrines it | W9: a second vendor re-derives it | Fix the spec; never loosen the tolerance |
| A borderline card makes the screen's outcome a rounding matter | Road §4.1's 30 % margin, checked by M4's re-derivation | Redesign the card |
| Derived ceremony lets a hazardous product stay `lite` | W6: safety requirements must name a hazard; the hazard table is cited | Raise-only; Gate I checks the derivation on all three tiers |
| Wood properties are taken as exact | W2: moisture basis and `[verify]`; creep factor cited | `make verify-list` |
| A pack task edits the core | W10 | A seam task, counted |

## 8. How the overseer runs this plan
As `v0.6-electrical-stage.md` §8, with these differences:
- Workers come from two pools: agy workers (any vendor's model the host's `agyctl` offers) for
  well-specified tasks, and the overseer's own client's subagents for design-heavy ones. Each task
  gets one worker in its own task folder (`make task`); the overseer reviews the diff and runs
  `make land`.
- Each wave's briefs carry W10's rule; after each wave the overseer records the `core/` count.
- The spec reproduction trial and the re-derivations use a different vendor's model from the one
  that wrote the spec.
- Push only when the maintainer asks.
