## How the waves have run (what worked)
- At most two subagents at once, each in its own `make task` folder; the overseer designs,
  briefs, reviews the diff and runs `make land` itself, one land at a time.
- Shared pieces two parallel tasks both need (a helper, a model field) land first as a small seam
  task, so neither waits on the other (S-251, S-252).
- Move-only splits are checked by the overseer for AST identity before landing (S-184 method).
- Briefs carry the decided design and the facts the task needs; reports are short; test output
  is kept short (`-q`, `| tail`).
