`BACKLOG.md` uses `merge=union` in `.gitattributes`.

- In commit `0fbbb24` (M2c), both `B-0191` and `B-0192` reappeared in `## Open` despite being in `## Closed`.
- A separate cleanup commit `d251c93` had to be landed to delete them from `## Open`.
- **Action:** Open `B-0206` to have `scripts/journal_ids.py` or `make land` automatically deduplicate rows between `## Open` and `## Closed`.
