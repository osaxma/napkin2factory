# Review gate D — UX in the owner persona (napkin2factory v0.4 "the tree", `main` @ 016689b)

Reviewer: fresh context, read-only, playing a hobby potter. Worked on copies under
`<scratch>/gate-d/` (`kiln`, `kiln-fail`, `demo`); nothing in the repo changed. `main` has since
gained a19a2e9 (a journal record only). Captures: `check.txt`, `check-all.txt`, `fail-check.txt`,
`fail2-check.txt`, `report.txt`, `context-door.txt`; screenshots `report-build*.png`,
`report-joints.png`, `sheet-{door,kiln,controls}.png`, `sheet-kiln-joints-crop.png`,
`assembly-html.png`, `demo-board-top.png`.

## Verdict: NOT READY (close: two blockers in the same two message strings, then text fixes)

The structure works for me. The build plan says plainly that the controls (step 1) and the door
(step 2) are put together on their own and then fitted in step 3. Each step has its parts table,
its "joints closed in this step" and the fixings counted per joint. The shortage message names
the screw and the gap ("4 missing, 34 listed where its joints need 38"). Promotion now says what
it did ("The product's structure starts from its 2 modules: door and controls, each still
empty"). Two things stop the release. (1) When parts do not line up at a joint, the one line the
owner reads in `n2f check`, `n2f status` and the report's "Next:" is all identifiers:
`part://outer-shell#1 and part://guard-shell#1 do not meet in asm://kiln: their frames are 5.0 mm
apart (allowed: 0.5 mm and 0.5°, offset 26 mm)`. (2) Even when everything passes, the new
alignment text puts `asm://kiln` into the requirements table and into every verdict of the new
"Joints between units" section. That section's verdicts are written for an engineer ("both
parties record the mating face; contact is allowed in the interference check (D29)"). Everything
else is a CONCERN.

## Q1 — Build plan section (`report-build-top.png`, `report-build-step3.png`)
What gets built separately, and in what order, is clear: "Step 1: Controls, built as one unit …
Fitted in kiln", "Step 2: Door …", "Step 3: Mini electric test kiln, the final assembly". The
fixings for each step are clear too ("4 × M6 female-female hex standoff, 25 mm, steel / 8 × M6 x
16 socket-head cap screw"), and the door and controls appear in step 3 as "step 2" / "step 1"
links. Problems:
- The lining is confusing. The text says only "Includes Lining: a grouping, not built separately."
  Nothing marks which of step 3's 26 items are the lining (items 18–26). "Lining" is capitalised
  like a name, and "grouping" means nothing to me.
- "Fitted in `kiln`" is the id in monospace, not "step 3".
- The door's reason is circular: "Built on its own because it is built as one unit, serviced or
  replaced as one unit." The controls' reason has two items with no "and" between them: "tested as
  one unit, made by another process or supplier".
- Step 3's joints come in id order, not build order. The controls joint is listed first but is
  section 3.7, the last one. Four rows read the same, "Inner sheet-metal shell to Ventilated touch
  guard"; only the monospace ids (`guard-standoff-top` …) tell them apart. The bottom one lists 4
  standoffs and no bolts, with no word that the stand's M6×50 bolts pass through them.
- The per-step check counts do not add up (see Q4).
Verdict: OK for order and fixings. CONCERN for the lining, reasons, "Fitted in kiln", joint labels.

## Q2 — Joints between units (`report-joints.png`)
The units column is good: "Mini electric test kiln and Door / fitted in step 3". So is the parts
column: "Hinge post (steel angle) (Mini electric test kiln) / Weld-on steel hinge … (Door)". The
verdict column does not read as information. Seen: "4 holes agree within 0.5 mm on both parties;
placed, the parts meet in asm://kiln (worst 0.00 mm)". On the hinges: "both parties record the
mating face; contact is allowed in the interference check (D29); placed, the parts meet in
asm://kiln (worst 0.00 mm)". The words "parties", "mating face", "interference check", "D29",
`asm://kiln` and "worst 0.00 mm" are all engineer words. The hinge joint is named "Hinge post
(steel angle) to Weld-on steel hinge", "how it is joined" says "faces that meet (welded, riveted or
clamped: no loose hardware)", and hardware says "none". So I cannot tell that this is *how the door
hangs on the kiln*. The intro paragraph ("does the part move relative to its neighbour … a reviewer
notes it as a decision") is aimed at a reviewer, which is acceptable. The joint column holds ids
only. Verdict: BLOCKER (verdict text, see 2). CONCERN for the joint names.

## Q3 — Assembly sheets (`sheet-door.png`, `sheet-kiln.png`, `sheet-kiln-joints-crop.png`)
A workshop would follow the tables. Neither I nor a workshop could tie a picture to an item: the
assembled and pulled-apart pictures carry **no item numbers (balloons)**, yet the footer says
"item numbers match the build plan and the BOM". The door's pulled-apart view shows the tray and
guard barely separated, with no hinges or latch visible. Jargon: "ITEMS (BOM LINES OF THIS STEP)",
column "BOM LINE", "PULLED APART (ITEMS MOVED ALONG THE EXPLODE AXIS)", the title block's
`asm://door`, and "DATE unsnapshotted". The known shrink is bad. On the kiln sheet the hinge rows
are set at font-size 1.59 (mm on an A3 sheet), where the other rows are 2.5 mm
(`grep font-size kiln.svg`: `2 font-size="1.59"`). That is about 64 % of the body text, and about
1.1 mm if the sheet is printed on A4. It is legible on screen only when zoomed
(`sheet-kiln-joints-crop.png`), and is the same row that already reads worst. For the welded
joints the sheet shows holes "—" and hardware "none". Verdict: CONCERN (balloons, shrink, labels).

## Q4 — Check output (`check.txt`, `check-all.txt`, `fail-check.txt`, `fail2-check.txt`)
- The by-unit grouping is plain: module column "Door, built as one unit", "Mini electric test
  kiln, the final assembly"; report "By module … Each unit is checked on its own".
- The new rows pass, so they are hidden by default. With `--all`: "the hardware for its 11
  joint(s) is all listed" (plain); "Door: its parts and their placement read only its own sizes,
  sizes above it and shared ones (7 built rule(s) looked at)" ("built rule" is jargon);
  `module-has-parts` detail "ok". Rule ids in the check column are unchanged from before
  (`general-v0/cots-source-verified@contactor`, `hardware://kiln`, `reach://door`).
- Empty-unit warning (demo): "Door has no parts yet: give it its parts, or fold it into the
  assembly above it". Plain. OK.
- **Worth a look: 66.** 24 "vendor link is marked unverified" (rule `cots-source-verified`), plus
  24 more rows on the same 24 parts ("vendor link not verified / did not resolve", the
  `validation@` rows), so every bought part appears twice. Another 18 say "BOM-only part: absent
  from the 3D model and the drawings", for bolts, nuts, standoffs and rivet nuts, which the new
  joint-hardware check now accounts for. So 42 of 66 rows are duplicates or things I cannot act on.
- Forced failure 1 (M6×16 38 → 34): exit 2, `needs attention │ M6 x 16 socket-head cap screw: 4
  missing, 34 listed where its joints need 38 (Ventilated touch guard to Controller enclosure; …
  Inner sheet-metal shell to Ventilated touch guard (3 joints))`. It says what is wrong. It does
  not say what to do: the fix ("list the missing hardware on the assembly's child lines") exists
  only in `--json` (`hardware.py:93`). The row also sits in the middle of the 66 worth-a-look rows.
  With 42 bolts listed instead: "4 more than needed …" (worth a look). Good.
- Forced failure 2 (guard-standoff-top guard frame shifted 5 mm): `part://outer-shell#1 and
  part://guard-shell#1 do not meet in asm://kiln: their frames are 5.0 mm apart (allowed: 0.5 mm
  and 0.5°, offset 26 mm)`. The same text is the report's "Next:" and the first bullet of
  `n2f status`. Nothing says which joint (top standoffs) in words, or what to do.
- Counts disagree for the door. Build plan: "Checks of this step: 10 OK · 3 example sign-offs".
  By-module table: 51 OK / 10 worth a look / 1 not checked. `n2f check --scope asm://door`: "49 OK
  · 6 worth a look · 1 not checked". The example sign-offs per step add to 5+3+5 = 13; there are 6.
Verdict: BLOCKER (failure 2 wording, see 1). CONCERN for noise, the missing "what to do", counts.

## Q5 — The assembly manual (`build/docs/assembly/page.md`, `assembly-html.png`)
The generated blocks sit well under the hand-written headings. "## 1. The controls" is followed by
"**Step 1: Controls, built as one unit.** Built on its own because …", the parts table and "Joints
closed in this step". No "phantom", "port", "driver" or `asm://` appears. Problems:
- **Contradiction.** Step 1's generated list says "Joints closed in this step: Controller
  enclosure to Controller enclosure lid: 4 × Self-tapping pan-head screw". The prose says "Leave
  the lid off until the element, the thermocouples and the door switch are connected in the final
  assembly (section 3.7)".
- The door's reason is circular, as in Q1.
- Step 3's lining line is unexplained: "It includes Lining: a grouping, not built separately."
- Step 3's joint list repeats the shell-to-guard line four times, and the two hinges each read
  "Hinge post (steel angle) to Weld-on steel hinge, 12 mm barrel, 60 mm (a joint between units):
  no hardware".
- The prose names each part twice ("the door tray (Door tray)", "the inner shell (Inner sheet-metal
  shell)"). This predates v0.4, but now that a table with item numbers sits above it, "(item 1)"
  would read better.
Verdict: CONCERN.

## Q6 — Concepts and promotion (`demo`, blank template)
`n2f init demo --template blank` → "next: sketch two directions — `n2f concept new "<title>"` — or
go straight to `n2f build`". Good. I wrote two cards, C-001 with `modules: door
{built-as-unit, serviced-as-unit}, controls {separate-process-or-supplier}`, as pm.md shows.
Lint, build and board were clean. The board shows "MODULES: door (built as one unit, serviced or
replaced as one unit); controls (made by another process or supplier)", which is plain. Promote:
"✓ C-001 is now the design direction (DEC-001). Seeded 7 parameters, 12 requirements (3 about
form, 2 about the alcove, 1 about the supply, 6 about module sizes) … The product's structure
starts from its 2 modules: door and controls, each still empty, inside the top assembly … snapshot
saved". This is clear. Size budgets read as plain requirements ("The controls module is at most
300 mm wide (computed from the rough shapes on C-001)"). But pm.md's own example massing draws the
door as a 3 mm plate, which became **"The door module is at most 3 mm deep"**. No real door with a
fibre plug can meet that. `n2f status` after promotion: "next: asm://controls has not been built
— run `n2f build`" (×3, raw ids). After the build: "next: no parts yet — the designer role
composes the kit". The step is right; the words are not plain. Verdict: OK for promotion.
CONCERN for the 3 mm budget and the status words.

## Blockers
1. The joint-alignment failure is all identifiers. Seen: `part://outer-shell#1 and
   part://guard-shell#1 do not meet in asm://kiln: their frames are 5.0 mm apart (allowed: 0.5 mm
   and 0.5°, offset 26 mm)`, in `n2f check`, as `n2f status`'s first bullet and as the report's
   "Next:". Wanted: "The inner sheet-metal shell and the ventilated touch guard do not line up at
   the top standoffs: their holes are 5.0 mm apart (0.5 mm allowed). Move the holes or the parts
   so they meet." Put the ids, the angle and the offset in `--json`. Use part names and the
   interface description for the joint. `src/napkin2factory/core/checks/interface.py:226,344,350-353`.
2. The passing alignment text and the mating-face text are not plain words, and they show on
   every run in the requirements table (REQ-011, REQ-012), the "Joints between units" verdicts and
   the check rows. Seen: "placed, the parts meet in asm://kiln (worst 0.00 mm)" and "both parties
   record the mating face; contact is allowed in the interference check (D29)". Wanted: "the 4
   holes line up on both parts, and in the assembled kiln (within 0.01 mm)" and, for welded joints,
   "the faces touch by design (welded, no loose fixings); they meet in the assembled kiln".
   `interface.py:176-178,361` (the "4 holes agree … on both parties" sentence just before it too).
