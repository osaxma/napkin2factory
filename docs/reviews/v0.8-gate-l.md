## Verdict: NOT READY

The loops themselves hold: promote, switch, undo, redo, rollback and abort each say what happened and how
to take it back, the refusals for a missing step, an unknown target and a stale approval name the command
that gets out, and the board and report open from `file://` with no console errors and no outside
requests. Five things block the release, because each shows Dana something wrong or leaves her with no
readable next step. (1) The example ships a lint error (S-275 removed the number from REQ-002), so after
every step `n2f status`, the report and `ecr close` name the stage "Intake" and print a raw lint line as
the next step. (2) The report's front tile reads "183 / 25 requirements OK". (3) An approved and closed
change (lower platen 100 → 125 mm) leaves "100 x 100 x 15 mm" in "what to tell the manufacturer" and in
the drawing's title block, which dimensions 125; the impact said nothing. (4) `n2f status` never mentions
an open change request; with a failing one open, its next step is
`REQ-002: needs attention: calc://capacity.load_cell_margin = 0.8 >= 1`. (5) `ecr close` on a
platen change wants all 13 safety sign-offs of the product (one of them a seeded duplicate), listed as one
semicolon paragraph of bare ids and `calc://` text with no reason per item. Fourteen concerns follow.
