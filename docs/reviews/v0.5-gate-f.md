## Verdict: NOT READY (four blockers; each fix is small)

The new owner surfaces mostly read well:
- the scope block in `n2f status` is plain;
- "Waiting on you" gives both options with what each takes, the cost and "(it holds the release)";
- `n2f decide` refuses a wrong pick plainly, and the newer-format refusal is exemplary;
- the bracket shows no scope noise, and the report opens from `file://` with no console errors.

Four things stop the release:
1. The choice I decided never reached the package. `n2f release` went through while `n2f status` said the change
   was "not yet applied"; the zip ships the M6 x 16 bolts I had chosen to replace.
2. The package's own `report.html` says "draft", gives no version, and says "no Revision for the current snapshot —
   n2f release vX.Y.Z", while its cover `README.txt` says "Revision v1.0.0".
3. For a wall shelf, the board's only problem line, and the requirement promotion writes, talk about an "open door"
   and an "alcove".
4. A product's own screen that fails while running prints an 87-line traceback.

## Q1 — Scope rows (`status-1.txt`, `ext*-status.txt`, `kiln-later`)

  wires it; the box keeps its cable entries (DEC-024)"; under Verify: "Electrical: later (DEC-024) — not checked at
  this stage; no requirement waits for it". The note says someone else does it, which reads like "done elsewhere". I
  cannot tell whether napkin2factory will design the wiring later, or never.
- **"no requirement waits for it" misleads.** It made me think nothing electrical is checked. Yet REQ-008, REQ-009,
  REQ-018 and REQ-019 (circuit, door switch, limit, earth bonding) are electrical safety requirements that still need
  sign-offs. They are not tagged `electrical`, so the line cannot say so (`stage.py:203-222` would add "… are about
  safety and still need a sign-off").
- **Nothing is silently gone, but status and check disagree.** I added REQ-028, tagged electrical.
  - `n2f status`: "1 requirement waits for it: REQ-028" (an id with no title).
  - `n2f check`: "REQ-028 │ Cable bend radius │ needs your sign-off", counted in the summary as "1 needs your
    sign-off". The report table says the same; its overview scope line does not name REQ-028.
  - The release went through without it: correct, but no output says so.
- **`state: in`** prints a bare "Electrical: in" (`manifest.py:32`).
- **`external`, no stub** (`n2f status` exits 1): "✗ ERROR (external) …/n2f.yaml: [scope.electrical] electrical is
  done elsewhere (`state: external`), but no module stands for it in this product → add model/assemblies/<id>.yaml
  with `build: external`, its `ports:` …". An agent can act on it. I cannot, and the rest of my status is gone.
- **`external` with a stub** (`asm://wiring`, port `iface://wiring-entry`): "Electrical: done elsewhere — …".
  - Before the first build: "Wiring (by the electrician): not built yet — run `n2f build`", for a thing never built
    here.
  - The build plan reads well: "Supplied, not built here / Wiring (by the electrician) / Wiring into the controller
    box".
  - The joints section does not: "Controls and no unit", "Wiring (by the electrician) (no unit)", "faces that meet,
    no loose fixings (welded, riveted or clamped)", "the faces touch by design". That is wrong for a cable entry.

## Q3 — A choice raised and decided (`status-1.txt`, `decide-1.txt`, `status-2.txt`, `release-*.txt`)
- **Readable in gist.** "Waiting on you: 1 choice / Bolts into both ends of the M6 female-female hex standoff, 25 mm,
  steel (it holds the release) / As listed, the M6 x 16 bolts, one into each end of a 25 mm spacer, meet inside it.
  14.5 + 15.0 = 29.5 mm of bolt in 25 mm at Door guard onto the door tray. …" I get it: the bolts collide inside the
  spacer. The cost of each option is there ("about the same (estimate …)", "about 4.80 USD less"), and so is the gate.
- **Words I could not follow:**
  - "female-female hex standoff", which then becomes "spacer" (the same part?);
  - "engages 10.5 to 11.0 mm of thread", "tapped right through", "plain spacer";
  - the sum "14.5 + 15.0" (where do these come from?).
  Option 1 holds only if "the spacer's thread must reach 11.0 mm from each end (a spacer tapped right through does)",
  and nothing says whether the spacer on my list is.
- **The printed command is not runnable.** `--pick shorter-bolts | through-bolt --by "<name>"`, pasted, gives
  `command not found: through-bolt` (exit 127) plus "Missing option '--by'". The options are numbered 1. and 2., but
  `--pick 1` gives "'1' is not an option … pick one of: shorter-bolts, through-bolt".
- **Release before deciding.** `n2f release v1.0.0` first refuses only for the example sign-offs ("REQ-003, REQ-007,
  … rely on EXAMPLE sign-offs", ids without titles). With `--allow-example-signoffs` comes a second refusal: "cannot
  release v1.0.0 yet: • a choice is waiting on you: … — `n2f decide …`". It is plain and gives the command, but the
  reasons come one at a time. The status and report "Next:" say only "replace the 6 example sign-offs … or release
  with --allow-example-signoffs", which leads straight into the choice refusal.
- **Deciding.** `n2f decide` printed "✓ DEC-025 recorded and saved: … shorter bolts at both ends: M6 x 12 / the check
  reads OK with this decision …", which sounds finished. Then `n2f status` dropped from "stage: Verify" to "stage:
  Design": "decided in DEC-025 (…), not yet applied — the designer makes the change: 32 M6 x 12 bolts …". Status does
  say who applies the change, but "the designer" is not a person I know (it is my agent). `n2f check` shows no row.
- **Releasing after deciding, before applying** (`kiln-unapplied`).
  - "✓ Revision v1.0.0 released". The zip's `bom.csv` has "M6 x 16 socket-head cap screw … 38" and "… 8", and no
    M6 x 12.
  - Afterwards `n2f status` reads "stage: Released" with "○ Design … not yet applied". The colliding bolts I decided
    against are now the frozen design.
  - Why: `gate_blockers` (`ecr/release.py:216-232`) reads gate blockers only. The unapplied pick is a caveat
    (`stage.py:419`, `choices.py:337-358`), "release not held [revisit]" in S-133.
- **Applying the change** (a new `m6x12-bolt`, four interfaces, two quantities) works: status is back at Verify;
  `check --all`: "the two M6 x 12 bolts stay 3.2 mm or more apart inside each 25 mm spacer".

Verdict: BLOCKER (1). CONCERN (11–15).
