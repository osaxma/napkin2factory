# Gate H: UX in the owner persona (napkin2factory 0.6, the wiring), commit f67dc06

## Verdict: NOT READY (two small blockers, then concerns)
Most of the wiring story already reads well to a potter. "Left for later" is stated plainly in
status, in the report and on the v1.0.0 package cover. The diff page says "Wiring: added". The
Wiring section opens with an honest "not reviewed by a licensed electrician" banner, and every
wiring warning ends "a licensed electrician decides". Nothing safety-relevant is folded away.
A product with no wiring never uses the word. Refusals are plain, with no stack trace.
Two things fail the owner:
- At the one step where the owner says yes, the impact report for the change request that adds
  the whole wiring reads "Impact of 0 change(s)" and "nothing is rebuilt".
- The wiring diagrams in the package (the files an owner hands to an electrician) carry no
  disclaimer, product name or revision.
Both are small fixes. The concerns are wording and presentation.
Captures: `/tmp/n2f-gate-h/*.txt`, `*.png`. Copies: `demo/`, `hand/`, `bracket/`, `err1-3/`,
`napkin/`.

## Q4. Engineering vocabulary: CONCERN (C6)

- Diagrams, schedule and labels: "PE" alone ("Earth terminal … terminal PE", "the supply

  for it". Scope note: "the box keeps its cable entries".
- Headings: "Control control circuit", "Earth bonding earth circuit · protected by not
  given", "Temperature sensing sensing circuit · 51 mV DC · protected by not given". These print
  the internal purpose, and "protected by not given" on the earth circuit reads alarming and
  broken. (C3)
- Labels: mostly good ("16 A (given)", "10.9 A, computed from 2500 W at 230 V", "Total length
  (computed)"). Exceptions: the lead says "Lengths are given in the wiring model" while every
  circuit note says "Lengths estimated from the placed parts" and the manual says "The lengths
  are estimates" (C4). Diff page "2500 W <= 3066.7 W" has no label. Units are present everywhere
  I looked.
