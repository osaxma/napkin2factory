### Confusions and Misleading Messages

1. **Loads block formatting on concept cards**:
   - `SPEC.md` §3 specified:
     > `loads: {point_load: 2000 N, max_deflection: 0.20 mm, cycles: 1000000}`.
   - When placed as top-level `loads:` in `concepts/C-001.yaml`, `n2f concept lint` failed with:
     > `✗ ERROR (C0-schema) concepts/C-001.yaml: [C-001] loads: Extra inputs are not permitted`
     > `→ remove or rename the key 'loads'; a card's keys are: id, title, status, based_on, refs, layout, envelope, site, style, modules, massing, notes, screening, decision`
   - When placed under `site.loads:`, `n2f concept lint` failed with:
     > `site.loads: Value error, expected a quantity like '800 mm' or a word like 'SA'`
   - The tool required the scalar keys (`point_load`, `max_deflection`, `cycles`) placed directly under `site:`.

2. **Concept card empty massing misleading guidance**:
   - Running `n2f concept build C-001` before defining massing produced:
     > `✗ C-001 has no massing yet — add kit calls under massing: in concepts/C-001.yaml, e.g. - {kit: enclosure, as: body, args: {length: 540 mm, width: 540 mm, height: 620 mm, thickness: 1.5 mm}}`
   - The example suggested `enclosure`, whereas for frame screening, the tool's structural screen specifically looks for `tube` or `block` pieces, or a `frame` with a top.

3. **Shroud flat blank vs 3D bounding box**:
   - `SPEC.md` noted: `guard shroud (polycarbonate 5 mm, line-bent U: back 350 + two sides 160 = 670 mm developed × 400 mm)`.
   - In massing, modeling the shroud as a flat developed plate (670 mm wide) caused `fits-site-width` to fail (`670 mm wide but the space is 400 mm wide`). The shroud in 3D is a bent U fitting 350 × 160 mm. Modeling it as separate plates/blocks representing the back and side panels satisfied both the 3D envelope and the `cnc-routing` area cost calculation (0.268 m²).

4. **COTS parts source and catalog schema validation**:
   - In `model/parts/*.yaml` for COTS parts, omitting `source:` produced:
     > `✗ ERROR (L7-cots-source) model/parts/actuator.yaml: [part://actuator] COTS part without a source`
     > `→ add source: {vendor, mpn, url, verified: false}; buyers need somewhere to buy it`
   - Using British English `'catalogue'` for `cost.basis` produced:
     > `cost.basis: Input should be 'estimate', 'quote' or 'catalog'`
   - The tool enforces American spelling `'catalog'`.

5. **Assembly children placement schema**:
   - Attempting to place items in `model/assemblies/*.yaml` with `at: {x, y, z}` (as in concept card massing) failed validation:
     > `✗ ERROR (model) model/assemblies/base-console.yaml: children.0.at: Extra inputs are not permitted`
   - Assembly child items accept only `part`, `asm`, `qty`, `spare`, `loose`; coordinates belong in geometry modules or auto-layout.

6. **Measurable statements unit check (`L1-measurable`)**:
   - Statements verified by `calc` require a recognized quantity with unit in the sentence:
     > `✗ ERROR (L1-measurable) model/requirements.yaml: [req://REQ-009] statement has no measurable value with a unit`
     > `→ write the limit into the statement, e.g. 'stays below 60 degC', or use a binary verify kind (interference/interface/manual/rule)`
   - Statements like "Factor of safety against yield of every frame member under rated load F is at least 2.0" lacked a unit (since safety factors are dimensionless). Adding the explicit load "2000 N" into the statement satisfies the linter.

7. **Tolerance class requirement (`L8-tolerance-class`)**:
   - Custom parts require explicit tolerance classes:
     > `✗ ERROR (L8-tolerance-class) model/parts/base-beam.yaml: [part://base-beam] custom part without tolerance_class`
     > `→ add tolerance_class: ISO2768-m (f/m/c/v); it goes on the drawing title block`

8. **Finish requirement on custom parts (`custom-part-complete`)**:
   - Custom parts without a `finish:` field passed lint but failed check:
     > `rule://general-v0/custom-part-complete@base-beam: part://base-beam is missing finish`
   - Adding `finish: anodized`, `finish: brushed-steel`, or `finish: plastic` resolved the check.

9. **Float vs Quantity in `.cases.yaml` tolerance (`abs:`)**:
   - In `model/calcs/structural.cases.yaml`, writing `abs: 0.1 N` caused check failure:
     > `calc-case://structural.load_cell_margin: could not convert string to float: '0.1 N'`
   - The `abs:` field expects a bare float in the expected unit (`abs: 0.1`), not a string with unit.

10. **Auto-layout overlap causing assembly interference**:
    - When no assembly geometry module is provided, `auto-layout` places children sequentially along the X-axis. Because step spacing was based on the preceding part's width rather than accommodating the succeeding part's half-width, placing a wide part (e.g. 340 mm `top-crosshead` or 100 mm `lower-platen`) immediately after a narrow part (e.g. 40 mm `column` or 80 mm `upper-platen`) resulted in bounding-box overlap:
      > `interference://portal-gantry: 1 pair(s) checked, 0 allowed contact(s), 1 interference(s): part://top-crosshead#1 ∩ part://upper-platen#1 = 8000.0 mm³`
    - Ordering children in the assembly YAML so that wider parts precede narrower parts completely eliminated overlapping under auto-layout.

## Item 10 confirmed: the auto-layout spacing is a package bug

`geometry/build_asm.py::_auto_layout` (the assembly without a geometry module, "placed in a row") puts each child's origin at `x`, then adds `bbox.x + 20` of that child. Parts are centred on their origin in x (built props: column min/max ±20, crosshead ±170, upper platen ±40, lower platen ±50), so the gap between two neighbours is 20 + half the first less half the second, and it is negative whenever a part is more than 40 mm wider than the one before it. Only the first child's width is ever used; the next child's half-width never is, and y and z are not looked at.

Reproduced: the trial product's `portal-gantry` with its children reordered narrow before wide (columns, crosshead, platens, base beam) goes from no interference to "3 pair(s) checked, 0 allowed contact(s), 3 interference(s)" (base beam against lower platen, 15000 mm³, and two more), and the final assembly to 5. In the agent's own order (wide first) the check is clean, which is why it found "wide parts first" as a workaround. The row is flagged "auto-layout: no geometry module; children placed in a row" as worth a look, yet the placeholder fails the interference check on its own, a failure no model fault caused. Filed: B-0251, about 30 minutes: advance by the previous part's `max` x less the next part's `min` x, plus the gap.
