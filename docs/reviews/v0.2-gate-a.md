# Review gate A — honesty and architecture (napkin2factory v0.2, `v0.2-build` @ b6a3589)

**Verdict: NOT READY — two small blockers, architecture sound.** The architecture holds: no build-graph rule can read `concepts/`, and empirically two cards + a board + deleting `concepts/` leave the manifest byte-identical with 0 rebuilt. Every screening number is labelled at the data level, the board renders 60/60 values with a badge, the CLI refuses red promotion and `--override` without `--reason`, and the override writes a `DEC-` quoting the failing rules with `verify: true`. Form and site requirements fail when violated and recover when restored, and the calc kit raises `DimensionalityError` on every wrong-unit call I tried. Two honesty holes must close before M5 writes the PM narrative on top of them: (B1) a card screens **green** on `circuit-available` yet its own promoted supply requirement **fails** at the first `n2f check` (headroom 1.2 vs 0.8 share — the napkin contradicts the factory on identical inputs); (B2) hand-editing `status: promoted` on an unbuilt card with no decision ticks the Napkin gate in `n2f status`, passes `n2f concept lint`, and locks both `promote` and `reject` — a stage marked complete by hand, against D10. Both are one-number / one-lint-rule fixes with a regression test each. Ten concerns follow for `BACKLOG.md`.

All runs were on tmp copies under the scratchpad (`q1`, `q3`, `q3c`, `q4`), via `tests.conftest.copy_product`; nothing in the repo was touched.
