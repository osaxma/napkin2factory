## Verdict: NOT READY for the owner (close — six text-level fixes and one behaviour fix)

The bones are right: making a variant is three steps and fifteen seconds, the red card's sentence ("it needs
1698 mm of width … but the alcove is 800 mm") is the best line in the product, every number on the board wears
a given/computed/estimate badge and the legend explains them in plain words, every refusal I hit was one plain
sentence with a next step and no stack trace, and no git word reached me. What stops me handing it to the owner:
(1) on the showcase kiln `n2f concept build --all` silently skips the three rejected cards, so the owner who
follows the README never sees the red card's output and the board shows three cards without pictures;
(2) the grey "where it comes from" lines under every number on the board and in `n2f concept show` are code
identifiers (`mass_cost.cost_estimate (… cots-controls …) [verify]`, `thermal_enclosure.heat_up_time`,
`massing bounding box (x)`, `h 5 W/m²K, ε 0.9`) — the board's most visible text after the numbers is for
engineers; (3) "about 0 kg: a one-person lift" for a 0.4 kg bracket; (4) the bracket's promotion summary reads
"Seeded 0 parameters, 0 requirements, updated style.yaml" plus a broken-grammar note, so I cannot tell what
changed; (5) `n2f status` after promotion says "stage: Intake" while ticking Intake; (6) `n2f init` tells me
"next: `n2f build`" when the README's first act is the napkin. None is deep; all are owner-facing.
