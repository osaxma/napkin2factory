# Review gate J (v0.7): UX in the owner persona

Reviewed: napkin2factory 0.6.0 (v0.7 work), commit 3139a00 ("M4: the test-stand benchmark product, Tier 2 (W9, S-222)"),
clean clone at <scratch>/repo. Persona: US hobby woodworker, inches and dollars, no
engineering background. Work copies are under <scratch>/work (mytable, table, table-imp,
table-ns, stand). Nothing was written in /Users/wakeel/napkin2factory.

## Verdict: NOT READY

The board and report do show imperial numbers with metric in brackets, and the lite table report is clearly shorter than the
standard one. But the napkin board gives this persona a green "holds up" for the flimsiest of her three ideas (bare legs, thin
pine top) with the sag check silently dropped. The dated regional cost promise is not visible anywhere, because the screen
reads a field the card does not have. The one manual lite relies on is written in newtons, kilograms and millimetres in an
imperial product. And a shop-facing line reads "50 x 50 x 0.0591 in" for a 50 mm tube. The test stand report is findable
(Safety is the second tab) but does not lead with the design, and its safety table is cut off at phone width.
Blockers B1-B4 below; concerns C1-C11.

## Q1. New table product, three ideas, imperial, structural rows on the board

Steps: `n2f init mytable --name "Dining table" --style appliance`; edited `n2f.yaml` to `units: imperial` and `region: US`
(`n2f init` has no flag for either); `n2f concept new` x3; filled 60 x 30 x 30 in cards (bare 1.5 in steel legs + 3/4 in
pine; 2 in steel apron frame + 1.5 in oak; steel plate trestle + 2.5 in oak) with `point_load: 330 lbf`, `edge_load: 110 lbf`,
`max_deflection: 0.1 in`, `budget: 800 USD`; `n2f concept build --all`; `n2f concept board`.

- First attempt `point_load: 330 lb` fails: `site.point_load is 330 lb, which is not a force -> give it as a force with its
  unit, like 1471.5 N`. The owner thinks "330 lb"; the hint names only newtons, not `lbf` (C1). `mass_limit: 150 lb` is
  accepted in the same card, so the same word means mass in one field and is refused in the next.
- Board, apron card C-002 (imperial display works): `point load 330 lbf (1468 N)`, `width 60 in (1524 mm)`,
  `mass of the shapes 120 lb (54.5 kg)`. OK.
- Structural rows of a PASSING card are not on the board face. They sit behind a collapsed toggle as bare numbers:
  `more numbers (5)` opens to `deflection 0.0372 in (0.944 mm)`, `yield factor 43.1`, `buckling factor 39.5`,
  `tip over factor 14.2`. No limit beside them, no sentence. Nothing reads like "sags 0.037 in under 330 lbf; the limit is
  0.1 in" (C2).
- A FAILING structural row (C-004: 1/2 in pine on the apron frame, limit set to 0.04 in, edge offset 12 in) reads on the board:
  `the top sags 0.0555 in (1.41 mm) (computed), beyond the 0.0402 in (1.02 mm) limit on the card (limit ÷ sag is 0.719, 1
  needed, static case) — FL^3 / (48*E*I) + 5*w*L^4 / (384*E*I) on the 30 in (762 mm) wide, 0.5 in (12.7 mm) thick top over a

  (85.3 N); Roark's Formulas ... calckit/data/materials.yaml [verify]`
  and `66.1 lb (30 kg) (the rough shapes' weight) with its centre 13 in (330 mm) inside the leg line, under 110 lbf (489 N)
  acting 12 in (305 mm) beyond it: factor 0.652 (computed), below the 2 needed (edge load case) — FS = (mass * g * cg_x) /
  ...`. The owner's 0.04 in limit comes back as "0.0402 in". The tip-over row never says "it tips over"; the problem list shows
  no rule title. Formulas, a repo file path and "[verify]" sit on the owner's card. No fix is offered: nothing says "thicker
  top, or add rails" or "move the legs out" (C3). The red card is information, but the fix is not obvious.
- Leg buckling: only in `concept show`/the toggle as "buckling factor 39.5" and a Johnson-parabola sentence with "slenderness
  72.7, K 2 (sway)... AISC 360 Commentary Table C-A-7.1". Not owner words (C3).
- BLOCKER B1. Idea 1, the bare-legs card, is built from tubes under a pine block. `n2f concept show C-001` ends

  column with an arm`, and the board shows nothing about it. A check that did not run must show on the card ("sag, tip-over and

- Budget row: C-002 amber `the 800 USD budget sits inside the cost band, 715 USD to 1146 USD`; C-003 red `even the low end of

- Promoting C-002 (copy `mytable-promoted`): `Seeded 0 parameters, 2 requirements (1 about form, 1 about the weight)`. The

## Q2. Where a cost comes from

- BLOCKER B2. Nothing on the board, in `concept show`, in the table report or in the stand report states the cost's region,
  date or staleness. Board: `rough cost of the apron frame and top from cost bands per process; to be checked`, then
  `a wide band: only the parts screening can price are counted`. Report: `980.35 USD per unit, from declared costs` and
  `Prices are declared estimates until a shop quotes them.` The data has it (`cost_bands.yaml`: `region: US ... as_of: "2026-10"`;
  stand parts: `basis: estimate, note: "mid-point of the cots-controls band ... US, 2026-10 [verify]"`), and the plan promises
  it (TRACK_A §3.2/§3.5). Cause: `src/napkin2factory/concepts/screens/generic.py:235` reads
  `card_region = getattr(card, "region", None)`, but the card model has no `region` attribute (`core/model/concepts.py`
  `ConceptCard`), so the region/date/stale branch at lines 273-290 never runs. I confirmed with `region: US` in `n2f.yaml` AND
  `site.region: US` on the card: the text stays unchanged. A stale band would also only show in the `elif` branch, so even that
  is unreachable for a product that gives a region. Fix: read the region from the card's `site.region`, else the product's
  `n2f.yaml` region, and add a test that builds a card and asserts the date appears. In the report, put region and date
  under the "Parts and cost" total in one line ("US costs, October 2026"), with "stale" only when stale.
- Tone: one disclaimer in the report ("declared estimates until a shop quotes them"), no lecture. OK. The board repeats
  "to be checked" under each card's cost; acceptable.
- Repeated findings (C4): the table report says `9 rule findings · 9 worth a look` and `3 not checked`. The nine are three
  issues said three times each: `part://leveling-foot: the vendor link is marked unverified`,
  `BOM-only part: absent from the 3D model and the drawings`, `vendor link not verified` (same three parts). The stand shows
  `27 worth a look`. The three "not checked" rows are form rules whose cause is the table's unset style
  (`style.yaml sets no corner_radius (0 mm)`). This is the "not checked" list the tool's voice rules out.

## Q4. Imperial display (copy `table-imp`: `units: imperial`)

- Board: inches first everywhere except volume, `volume of the shapes 26.4 l` (C9). OK otherwise.
- Report requirement and parameter tables: `table-length 63 in (1600 mm)`, `mass-limit 154 lb (70 kg)`,
  `centre-load 331 lbf (1471.5 N)` (the owner's 330 lbf comes back as 331). OK.
- BLOCKER B4. Dimension lists with one trailing unit convert only the last number: Parts table, "what to tell the
  manufacturer": `welding; 50 x 50 x 0.0591 in (1.5 mm) square tube, S235` and `the tabs are 40 x 40 x 0.157 in (4 mm) plate`.
  An inch reader sees a 50 x 50 inch tube. Cause: `QTY_PATTERN` in `src/napkin2factory/report/units.py` (line ~28) converts
  "number + unit" pairs only. Fix: convert `A x B x C unit` as a group, or leave the whole group in mm. Also
  `0.0591 in` and `0.157 in` are not how a shop speaks (C9: round to 1/16 in or give the gauge).
- `n2f concept build`, `n2f concept show`, `n2f check`, and the requirement written at promotion stay metric on an imperial
  product: `✓ C-001 holds up: 1524 × 762 × 762 mm (computed), about 19.9 kg (computed)`; `point load 1468 N`; the check table
  `total_mass = 45.2439 kg <= 70 kg`. The persona sees these in the terminal and in her agent's replies. Say so in the guide,
  or convert (C9).
- Where is "inches on the screen, drawings in mm" stated for an owner? Only in the agent guide's last reference list
  (`docs/agent-guide.md:313`: `drawings stay in mm`). The report's Drawings section says only `Click a drawing to enlarge it;
  the full-size files are in build/drawings/.` The drawing title block does say `UNITS mm` and `Dimensions in mm`, and the hole
  tables show `⌀9.0`, `70.0` unlabelled. An owner would find it only by looking at the title block (C10).
- Discoverability (C10): `n2f init` has no `--units`/`--region`; `roles/pm.md`, the product `AGENTS.md` and the card skeleton
  never mention inches or ask for units; the skeleton's examples are `800 mm`, `1600 USD`, `40 kg`. A US owner gets a metric
  board unless the agent finds `units:` in the guide's last section (line 313). Add one PM question ("inches or millimetres?")
  and a flag.

## Blockers (fix before the next milestone)

B1. Green "holds up" board card with an unrun structural check (the bare-legs idea). Seen: `· sag stays within the limit: not
  run: the column and the arm are different materials`, board `screening found no limitations`. Wanted: a card whose sag,
  tip-over or buckling check did not run must say so on its face, in owner words, and not read as an all-clear; ideally the
  generic structural screen handles mixed-material column-and-top (its message is `src/napkin2factory/concepts/screens/` the
  structural screen; the board's verdict wording is in `src/napkin2factory/concepts/board.py`). Fix: add a "not checked"
  amber line to the board for skip-with-reason on a structural rule when the card gives a load, and add a test with the
  bare-legs card.
B2. Cost region, date and staleness never appear (board, `concept show`, report, stand report). Seen: cost source text
  `rough cost of the apron frame and top from cost bands per process; to be checked`. Cause:
  `src/napkin2factory/concepts/screens/generic.py:235` `getattr(card, "region", None)`; the card has no such attribute. Fix: read
  `site.region` or the product region; show "US, 2026-10" in the source sentence; add the line to the report's cost total.
B3. The one lite manual (and any manual text) is metric in an imperial product. Seen: `490.5 N (50 kg)`, `45.244 kg in all`,
  `50 mm in from every edge`, `a couple of centimetres`. Where: `examples/table/model/docs/build-and-care.md` (hard-coded `(50 kg)`,
  `(150 kg)`, "centimetres"), `src/napkin2factory/docs/engine.py:113` (`bom.total_mass`), the `P[...]` rendering in the manual
  engine (does not call `src/napkin2factory/report/units.py`). Fix: render quantities in manuals through the display-units
  helper and write the example text without literal units.
B4. Wrong-looking dimensions: `50 x 50 x 0.0591 in (1.5 mm) square tube`, `40 x 40 x 0.157 in (4 mm) plate`.
  `src/napkin2factory/report/units.py` `QTY_PATTERN` (~line 28). Fix as in Q4.
