## Confusions and Misleading Messages

1. **Frame generator bare legs restriction vs concept spec**:
   - The spec states for C-001: *"Legs: 4 × 40 × 40 × 2 mm square tube, mild steel... No rails."*
   - Attempting to model this in massing with `kit: frame` and `top_rails: false, bottom_rails: false` raised:
     > `"ValueError: a frame needs top_rails or bottom_rails: bare legs are not a weldment"`
   - Modeling with individual `kit: tube` parts caused the `structural` screen to skip the table checks:
     > `"the rough shapes show no frame with a top or column with an arm: nothing carries the load"`
   - To make the structural screen recognize the table and compute the 14.3 mm sag as expected by the spec, `frame` had to be used with `top_rails: false, bottom_rails: true`.

2. **COTS hardware parts listed in interface `interfaces:`**:
   - For `mounting-bolt-m8` and `wood-insert-m8`, adding `iface://top-to-frame` to their `interfaces:` list caused `n2f validate` to fail:
     > `"ERROR (interface) model/parts/mounting-bolt-m8.yaml: [part://mounting-bolt-m8] lists iface://top-to-frame but that interface's parties are ['part://apron-frame', 'part://top-board']"`
   - Hardware parts specified on an interface's `spec.hardware` must not list the interface in their own `interfaces:` list; they only belong in the assembly children and the interface's `hardware:` list.

3. **`n2f lint` requirement statement regex on dimensionless factors of safety**:
   - Writing requirement statements without explicit unit quantities (e.g., *"Yield factor in every spanning member under centre load is at least 1.5."*) caused `n2f lint` to fail with:
     > `"ERROR (L1-measurable) model/requirements.yaml: [req://REQ-007] statement has no measurable value with a unit"`
   - Even though a factor of safety is dimensionless, the lint rule requires an explicit unit string in the statement text, requiring statements to explicitly mention the load unit (e.g. `1471.5 N`).

4. **Bolt-pattern rect spacing requirement on interfaces**:
   - Specifying `pattern: rect, count: 6` on `top-to-frame` without `spacing:` caused `n2f build` to fail:
     > `"part://apron-frame: applying interfaces failed: ValueError: a rect pattern of 6 holes needs spacing"`
   - Unlike count 1, even counts in rect patterns require an explicit 2-element `spacing: [dx, dy]`.

5. **Reference cases `abs` tolerance type**:
   - Specifying `abs: 0.05 mm` in `structural.cases.yaml` failed during `n2f check` with:
     > `"could not convert string to float: '0.05 mm'"`
   - `abs` tolerance in calc reference cases expects a dimensionless float representing the tolerance magnitude in the expected unit, rather than a unit string.

6. **Multi-instance hardware multiplier calculation in BuildUnits**:
   - For `feet-to-frame`, specifying `pattern: rect, count: 4` with `per_hole: 1` when the table had 4 `leveling-foot` child parts caused `n2f check` to compute demand as `16` instead of `4`:
     > `"M10 leveling glide: 12 missing, 4 listed where its joints need 16 (Leveling feet to leg threaded inserts)."`
   - Because `BuildUnits.joints` multiplies occurrences by hole count (`qty = occ * (h.per_hole * holes)`), each foot occurrence was multiplied by 4 holes. Setting `count: 1` per foot resolved the demand to 4.
